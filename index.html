Project 02
Civic Samadhan — AI Civic Problem Solver
A platform that connects people who report civic problems with those who can solve them: citizens report, universities build solutions, industry partners fund and mentor, and government verifies results. I built the AI service, a standalone Python FastAPI microservice with two pipelines. The first reads a text or photo report, assigns a category and a 0–10 priority score, detects duplicates and groups similar issues. The second ranks proposed solutions on problem fit, cost, time, past experience and profit, showing the best one first with reasons. Backed by 33 automated tests.
